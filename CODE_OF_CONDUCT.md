# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit the work and data of others correctly.
- Report results honestly, including negative ones.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Fabricating, falsifying or misrepresenting data or results.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **sudiptakumar400@gmail.com**. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban a participant whose behaviour is harmful.
